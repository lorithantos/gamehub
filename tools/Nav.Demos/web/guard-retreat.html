What the doctrine decided

    Three things to watch

    The line never empties. Only the damaged unit leaves. The other three keep their cells, so the position is held the whole time one of them is away.

    It comes back. A unit at the pad is still a member of the squad. Once it is repaired the doctrine recalls it and it takes a place in the formation again.

    Two casualties, two pads. Unit 0 is hurt at tick 88, while unit 2 is still repairing at the south pad. It is sent to the north one instead: the doctrine passes over a pad a fellow is already heading to, so two casualties never queue at the same one.
