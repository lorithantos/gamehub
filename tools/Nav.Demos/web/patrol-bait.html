What the doctrine decided

    Three things to watch

    All three, or none. The doctrine has one move, and it takes every unit on station. There is no verb for sending one after something, so a lure cannot pick off a straggler.

    The shaded band is the rule. A hostile inside it is worth engaging; outside, it is somebody else’s problem. The lure is inside when it appears and outside once it runs.

    The route resumes. Breaking off is not a special retreat. With nothing to engage, the next waypoint is simply where the patrol was going anyway.
